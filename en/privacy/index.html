YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy Policy

What Device CpuX reads, what it stores locally, and what happens during network requests and sharing.

Effective / last updated: 2026-10-29 · Device CpuX

No account required. Device readings and diagnostic results are processed locally. Local history, widget snapshots, startup requests, and sharing you initiate are explained below.

1. Scope and developer

This policy covers the Device CpuX iPhone app, its companion Home Screen widgets, and this website, provided by zhangzheng. For privacy questions, contact 18260061607@163.com.

2. Device information and diagnostics

The app uses public iOS interfaces to read and display device model, system version, processor, graphics capabilities, battery, memory, storage, thermal state, and connectivity on your device. The app does not automatically upload these readings or diagnostic results to developer servers. No account is required. The app does not integrate advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs for cross-app tracking, and does not actively read IDFA, IMEI, or device serial numbers. Some specifications come from DeviceKit model reference data and may differ from live system readings.

3. Local storage, retention, and deletion

  • Preferences: language and appearance settings are stored on your device.
  • Recent tests: diagnostic counts, network test hostnames and performance results, touch coverage results, and test times are stored locally. Each category retains up to 12 recent entries; newer entries replace older ones.
  • Widgets: the main app shares its latest snapshot with its widgets through an on-device App Group. This includes model, system version, battery level, storage, physical memory, charging and thermal state, language, and update time. A newer snapshot replaces the previous one.
  • Sensors: live location, motion, pressure, step, and microphone samples support the active feature. The app does not store raw sensor tracks, audio recordings, or camera images.
  • Deletion: use the clear test data action in the data section of app Settings to remove recent test records and current network test results. This does not also clear language, appearance, or widget snapshots. Deleting the app removes its app-container data; iOS manages cleanup of shared widget data. Local app data may also be included in system backups you control.

4. Permissions and sensors

MicrophoneMeasure live input level and waveform in memory without saving recordings.

You can deny or revoke permission in iOS Settings. The related feature may become unavailable; features that do not need that permission remain available. Live sensor tests stop when you leave their screen or the app enters the background.

5. Startup requests, network tests, and cookies

Startup endpoint: once per app process launch, the app sends a GET request to https://h5-rest.vfmoney.net/api/h5/v1/auth/csrf to obtain a CSRF session response. The app does not attach device readings, location, diagnostic results, or advertising identifiers. The connection itself exposes metadata such as IP address, request time, and request headers to the endpoint and network providers. The system network session may receive, store, and send cookies returned by the endpoint. This metadata and these session cookies are distinct from uploading diagnostic data. The endpoint may process access logs for service delivery, security, and troubleshooting; specific log fields, retention periods, and cookie lifetimes depend on server configuration and can be confirmed through the developer contact.

Network tests you initiate: only after you start a test does the app request your selected HTTPS URL; the default is Apple’s website. The destination handles request metadata, including IP addresses, under its own policy. Tests use network traffic. The app uses an ephemeral network session for these tests.

6. Sharing, support emails, and third parties

The app does not automatically sell or share device readings, sensor data, or test history with advertisers. If you use the system share sheet to export a summary, your chosen recipient receives the content you share and handles it under its own policy. If you contact support, the developer uses your email address, description, and any attachments you provide to respond. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, or unrelated personal information. Support emails are retained to resolve requests and necessary follow-up; you can request deletion through the contact address. DeviceKit provides model and specification information; Alamofire performs network requests. Their inclusion does not mean diagnostic data is uploaded to their authors.

7. Website visits

This is a static website. It sets no analytics cookies, loads no ads or third-party analytics scripts, and does not read your in-app diagnostic history. Website hosting or network providers still receive connection metadata such as IP addresses, browser request headers, and access times, and may retain access and security logs under their own policies. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The website has no registration, comment, or file-upload features.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage permissions in system Settings, clear recent test data in the app, or stop using and delete the app. There are no user accounts to close. The developer cannot retrieve test records from a server when those records are stored only on your device. For support emails or server information identifiable to you, contact the developer to request access, correction, or deletion. Requests will be verified and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Children, processing locations, and security

The app is not designed to collect children’s personal information and does not request dates of birth. Contact the developer if a child has provided personal information through support. Diagnostics and sensor data are processed on your device. Email, sharing, website hosting, and startup endpoint services may process network information in the regions where those services operate. Network requests use HTTPS. System permissions and app sandboxing control local access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes and contact

If data handling changes, this page will be updated with a revised effective date. Where needed, notice will be provided in the app or through another appropriate channel. For questions about this policy or privacy, contact zhangzheng at 18260061607@163.com.
